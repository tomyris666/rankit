Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our SEO services

How long does it take to see results from SEO?

SEO is a long-term strategy, and results typically take time to materialize. While some improvements may be noticeable within a few weeks (such as technical fixes), significant ranking improvements and traffic increases usually take 3-6 months. The timeline depends on various factors, including your website's current state, competition level, and the aggressiveness of your SEO strategy.

What makes your SEO services different from others?

At RankIT Consultancy, we take a data-driven, transparent approach to SEO. We focus on sustainable, white-hat strategies that deliver long-term results rather than quick fixes that could harm your website in the long run. Our team stays up-to-date with the latest algorithm changes and industry trends, and we provide detailed reporting so you can see exactly what we're doing and the results we're achieving.

Do you guarantee first-page rankings?

We don't guarantee specific rankings because search engine algorithms are constantly evolving and rankings can fluctuate for reasons outside our control. Any SEO agency that guarantees #1 rankings is not being honest. What we do guarantee is that we'll use industry best practices, transparent reporting, and continuous optimization to improve your search visibility and drive more qualified traffic to your website.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?

While SEO is a long-term strategy and the best results come from consistent effort over time, we understand that businesses need flexibility. We offer month-to-month contracts as well as discounted rates for longer-term commitments. We're confident in our ability to deliver results, which is why we don't lock clients into lengthy contracts.

How do you measure SEO success?

We measure success through various metrics, including organic traffic growth, keyword rankings, conversion rates, and ROI. At the beginning of our partnership, we'll work with you to establish specific KPIs based on your business goals. Our regular reports will track these metrics and provide insights into the performance of your SEO campaign.
